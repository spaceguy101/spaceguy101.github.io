About

Shreyas Ponkshe

Lead engineer at Velotio (R Systems), currently on a Netflix engagement. Backend and cloud for a decade, now spent on production GenAI. Twice a co-founder.

Personal

I'm a backend engineer and a two-time co-founder, based in Pune, India. I mostly write Node.js, TypeScript, and Python, and I have spent most of the last ten years somewhere between an API and the infrastructure underneath it.

For the last few years that has meant production GenAI. The GenAI part is less a pivot than the same job with a new failure mode: retrieval and agents are still systems that have to hold up under load, cost money to run, and be observable when they go wrong.

Co-founding two companies means I have owned the infrastructure bill alongside the architecture. Running a compute-heavy pipeline on a startup budget set most of my instincts about what to build and what to buy.
